Who we are

Small steps.
Lasting values.

Scouting is a journey of learning by doing: building confidence, looking out for one another, and discovering how each of us can make a difference.

At Scout National Libanais, that spirit brings adventure, friendship and service together. Whether you are discovering scouting for the first time or are already part of the community, you are welcome here.

The scouting spirit

More than a day outdoors.

01 / EXPLORE

Find your sense of adventure

Step outside, notice the world around you, and develop a deeper respect for nature.

02 / CONNECT

Grow together

Learn through teamwork, share new experiences, and build friendships along the way.

03 / CONTRIBUTE

Make a difference

Practice kindness, take responsibility, and bring a spirit of service into everyday life.
